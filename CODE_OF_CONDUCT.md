# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Next.js Blog System is a source available publishing system for people who want to build and run their own blogs. We want the project repository and its community spaces to be useful, respectful, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds, identities, and levels of experience.

By participating in this project, you agree to help maintain that environment.

## Where this applies

This Code of Conduct applies to the project repository, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, commit messages, and documentation. It also applies to the project website and to public or private spaces where someone is representing Next.js Blog System or its maintainers.

People running their own installation may adopt this policy for their blog community, but this document does not give the project maintainers control over those independent deployments.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Treat people with respect, patience, and good faith.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project context without gatekeeping.
- Discuss implementation choices without attacking the people who made them.
- Give feedback that is specific, constructive, and relevant to the project.
- Respect the privacy of authors, readers, customers, and contributors.
- Keep passwords, access tokens, private posts, private configuration, and deployment details out of public issues and pull requests.
- Follow the project's [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md), license, and security reporting process.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, unwanted sexual attention, or sexual content directed at another participant.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated inflammatory behavior.
- Publishing or sharing private or identifying information without permission.
- Publishing credentials, private blog content, customer data, or deployment information without authorization.
- Misrepresenting another person's identity, work, consent, or relationship to the project.
- Pressuring someone to disclose private account, customer, or deployment access.
- Unwelcome contact after someone has asked for it to stop.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider seriously harmful, abusive, or unprofessional in a project community.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness behavior that may violate this Code of Conduct, please contact the repository owner privately through GitHub: [@ketankauntia](https://github.com/ketankauntia). State that the message is a Code of Conduct report and include:

- What happened and when it happened.
- Links to the relevant issue, pull request, comment, message, or page.
- The GitHub usernames or other names involved.
- Any context or witnesses that may help explain the situation.
- What support or follow-up you would find helpful.

Please do not include passwords, access tokens, private posts, customer data, or other sensitive material unless it is necessary to explain the report. For suspected security vulnerabilities, use the private security advisory process described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) instead of filing a public issue.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Details will be shared only with people who need them to review and respond. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.

## How we respond

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They will review reports fairly, consider the safety and privacy of everyone involved, and give relevant people an opportunity to provide context when appropriate.

Depending on the circumstances, a response may include a private reminder, a warning, removal or editing of content, temporary limits on participation, or permanent removal from project spaces. Serious or repeated violations may result in immediate action. Maintainers may close or reject contributions that breach this policy.

If a report concerns a maintainer, that person will not be the sole decision-maker. The project may ask a trusted independent reviewer to help assess the report.

## Questions

Questions about this Code of Conduct can be sent privately to the repository owner through [GitHub](https://github.com/ketankauntia). This document may be updated as the project and its community grow.
